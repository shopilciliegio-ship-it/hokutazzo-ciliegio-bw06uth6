How it works

A path from the vine to the glass

Our proposals are designed for groups of 15 people or more. We welcome you ourselves, as a family, and tell you about the farm, its origins and our daily work.

We are ten minutes from the centre of Siena and from Monteriggioni castle, two minutes from the expressway exit towards Florence, in open countryside: convenient for coaches too.

The itinerary

What you will see

  1. Welcome to the farmWe introduce the farm: its origins, the work, past and present.
  2. Vineyards, animals and vintage toolsThe tour starts in the vineyards, passes the farm animals and the old vintage tools, and ends in the cellars.
  3. The cellarWe explain the wine-making process, in more or less detail depending on your group’s questions and curiosity.
  4. The barrel roomFrom fermentation to ageing in wooden barrels, with an aperitif of sweet and dry sparkling wine.
  5. The vinsantaiaIf the group wishes, a visit to the vinsantaia with an explanation of the traditional production techniques.
  6. TastingInside the restaurant, with the options below.
