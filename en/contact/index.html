Our location

How to find us

Società Agricola Il Ciliegio
Via Uopini 94
53035 Monteriggioni (Siena)

From Florence or Siena take the Siena–Florence expressway and exit at Badesse. Follow the signs for Uopini: just past the village of Badesse, after about two hundred metres, you will see the sign for our farm on the left.

Ten minutes from the centre of Siena and from Monteriggioni castle, two minutes from the expressway exit.
