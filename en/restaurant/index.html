The formula

Simple and captivating

The first group to book decides that day’s menu, and whoever arrives later eats what the first has chosen.

Simple, because it lets us take better care of each course: in the kitchen we concentrate on a few dishes. It also keeps costs down, which would otherwise end up on our guests’ bill.

Captivating, because many people enjoy coming to us without knowing what they will eat at lunch or dinner, perhaps delighted by the sight of a succulent, unexpected Florentine steak.

Our table

A place where everyone is on first-name terms

A new building in full old style, warm and welcoming, where after a few minutes everyone ends up chatting with people they have never met. The service is family-run.

The menus are built from a long list of our own products, almost all sourced directly at the farm or from neighbouring producers: an added value that brings back flavours we were losing sight of. Everything is accompanied by wines of our own production.
