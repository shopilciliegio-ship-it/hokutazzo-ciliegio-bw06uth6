Ceremonies & meetings

A special day, leaving nothing to chance

Il Ciliegio is the ideal place for weddings, communions, graduations and banquets of all kinds, or simply for a few hours of good cooking, clean air and great wines.

Guests are welcomed by us and accompanied to the various rooms according to the day’s programme. While the aperitif is served in the area where the wines are aged, guests can wander freely around the cellar and the farm, including the animals and the museum of farm machinery.

The programme continues in the restaurant, where our women skilfully prepare produce that almost entirely comes from the farm or from neighbouring producers. Everything is accompanied by our own wines, served directly by us as a family, and often with a little good music and fireworks.

The spaces

Two halls, a veranda and a lawn

The two halls and the terrace offer many set-up options: ceremonies for over 200 guests, on the outdoor terrace (which can be covered with a marquee) or indoors if the weather is bad.

Indoor halls

  • Two separate halls, 220 seats

  • Step-free

Veranda and lawn

  • Marquee to cover the terrace

Groups & coaches · from 15 people

A convenient stop for tour operators and agencies

We are just ten minutes from the centre of Siena, two minutes from the expressway exit towards Florence, ten minutes from Monteriggioni castle, in open countryside: a strategic spot for coaches and groups.

  • Guided visit: vineyards, animals, vintage tools, cellar and barrel room
  • Tastings and tourist menus, even with several coaches at once
  • Very fast service for those short on time
  • Over 200 indoor seats
  • Guides in Italian, English, French, Russian, Romanian and Greek
