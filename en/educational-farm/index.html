Why come to the farm

The main reason people visit farm stays is to find again that part of ourselves we have lost who knows where, swept along by the pace of daily life: to rediscover nature, animals, and also people, whom we too often see in the wrong light.

Seeing families arrive with children or grandchildren to feed the farm animals, to visit the museum of vintage tools that grandpa used when he was young, and perhaps to let the kids grind wheat to discover how bread used to be made, fills us with pride and gives us the drive to keep going down this road.
