Il Ciliegio: a family, its land and its wine, since 1952

We farm almost 100 hectares in the Chianti Colli Senesi, with vines, olive trees and arable land. Come and see the winery, sit at our table, or have our products shipped to your home.

Since 1952, the same family

From the Veneto to the hills of Siena

In the early 1950s our patriarch Mario left the Veneto and brought his wine culture to Tuscany. Today we are still here, all together as a family: from the fields to the cellar, from the kitchen to sales.

From the vine to the glass

A guided visit to the vineyards and the winery, then we taste: four wines and Vin Santo, also with cured meats and cheeses. For groups, schools and families, with our educational farm.
