Since 1952, the same family

From the Veneto to the Sienese hills

In the early 1950s our patriarch Mario left the Veneto, the Euganean Hills, and brought his wine-making culture to Tuscany. With his four sons he laid the foundations of the farm. At the end of the 1980s the farm specialised in viticulture, with its own winery and bottling. In 2008 the educational farm stay and the restaurant were born.

Today we are still here, all in the family: each of us has a part of the work, from the fields to the cellar, from the kitchen to sales.
