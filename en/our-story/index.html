Our story

Since 1952, the same family

From the Veneto to the Sienese hills

Il Ciliegio is in the heart of Tuscany, at the centre of Chianti and a stone’s throw from the marvellous city of Siena. For some seventy years we have been part of the local wine scene, known by many for our historic activity: the production and direct sale of high-quality wine and olive oil.

Our story tells of the hard work of the patriarch Mario, who in the early 1950s moved from the Veneto to Tuscany, bringing with him a good baggage of wine-growing culture learned in his native land: the Euganean Hills, also an area of fine wines.

In the following years, helped by his four sons, Mario laid the foundations of the farm. The productive turning point came at the end of the 1980s: the farm specialised in viticulture and focused on the cellar, bottling and sale of the wines produced on the farm, with renovation of the buildings and new oenological techniques.

In 2008 the educational farm stay and the restaurant were born, to offer visitors the pleasure of immersing themselves for a few hours in the farming world, with its flavours and its slow pace.

The vineyards

It is in these very soils, in the Chianti Colli Senesi, that the nobility of Sangiovese, in a successful marriage with other superior grape varieties, brings the winemaking excellence of Tuscany into the bottle. On these hills we farm 100 hectares of land, of which almost 50 are specialised vineyard and about 15 intensive olive grove: wine and olive oil of great quality.

The vines thrive here and meet the stony ground, ideal for Chianti vines. The medium-textured soil, with a balanced mix of clay, silt and sand and moderately calcareous at the surface, ensures good drainage: water filters through without pooling and keeps the roots moist. The Mediterranean climate, with little rain in summer and plenty in autumn and winter, and the right placement of the vineyards between north-west and south-east complete the picture.

Intuition and the right strategies convinced us that to grow you must respect the right timing: each vineyard has its own identity and its own story.

The vineyards are organised with yield quality in mind. Good practice advises not asking too much of the oldest plantings; densities rise for the newer ones, where the Guyot and single-sided spur cordon training systems are used.

The company philosophy is the same as ever: the will and commitment to produce quality wines.

How to find us

You will find us at Via Uopini 94, Monteriggioni, province of Siena. From Florence or Siena take the Siena–Florence expressway and exit at Badesse; follow the signs for Uopini: just past the village of Badesse, after about two hundred metres, you will see the sign for our farm on the left.
